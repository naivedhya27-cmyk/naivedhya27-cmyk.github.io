Things we have built

Most of it was built for client work; the rest are products and benchmarks we started ourselves. The full list is on the work page.

  • The operations agent. An AI coding agent that runs our delivery from short instructions, with house rules, client context and the state of each workstream kept in files it reads every session.

  • A clinical note reader. A 135M-parameter model, delivered to a hepatology client, that turns liver-injury notes into structured fields on a laptop CPU.

  • PramaanBench. An open benchmark, in build, of frontier models on Indian health, law and farming questions made from real records.
